Privacy Policy

BandUp: IELTS Prep · Last updated: 18 November 2026 (iCloud sync)

In short: BandUp does not collect, store on servers, sell or share any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Everything you do in the app stays on your devices and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud account.

Data stored on your device

Your profile (optional first name, exam type, target band, exam date), practice results, saved vocabulary and review schedule are stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData. We (the developer) cannot access this information.

iCloud sync

If you are signed in to iCloud, your progress is synced between your own devices through your private iCloud database (Apple CloudKit). This data is held by Apple under your Apple ID and is not accessible to us. You can turn iCloud off for BandUp in Settings → [your name] → iCloud.

Microphone and speech recognition

Speaking practice uses the microphone and Apple's Speech framework to transcribe your answer so the app can give feedback. Recognition runs on-device whenever your device supports it. On some devices Apple may process audio on its servers under Apple's Privacy Policy. BandUp does not save or upload recordings. You can type your answer instead and deny these permissions at any time in Settings.

AI feedback

On supported devices, Writing and Speaking feedback uses Apple Intelligence models that run on your device. Your essays and transcripts are not sent to us or to any third-party AI service.

Apple Watch

A small progress summary is sent from your iPhone to your paired Apple Watch over Apple's WatchConnectivity. It never leaves your devices.

Links

The app contains links to external websites (for example ielts.org and Wikipedia). Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Children

BandUp collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
