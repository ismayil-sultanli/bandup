FAQ

Are these official IELTS tests?
No. Tests follow the real IELTS format (Listening 4 parts / 40 questions, Reading 3 passages / 40 questions) but are written for BandUp or adapted from openly licensed sources. Band scores are practice estimates.
The listening audio sounds robotic.
Download free higher-quality voices: Settings → Accessibility → Read & Speak (Spoken Content) → Voices → English → choose voices marked Premium or Enhanced. BandUp uses them automatically.
Speaking practice doesn't hear me.
Allow Microphone and Speech Recognition for BandUp in Settings → Privacy & Security, or switch on “Type my answer instead”.
Does it cost anything?
No. BandUp is free, with no ads and no account.
